MZJ Haraj Manager v1.8.0 CLEAN

CORE RULES
- One Haraj account for the company.
- One company daily limit.
- Fill each publishing day up to available daily capacity before moving to the next day.
- Split the day's ads across active branches according to active rep count.
- Split each branch share only across reps of that branch.
- No duplicate vehicle group inside a coverage cycle.
- Agency-location stock remains excluded.

AD COPY / COMPAREKEY
- Interior specs: CompareKey only.
- Exterior specs: CompareKey only.
- Safety specs: CompareKey only.
- Missing/not-found/incomplete CompareKey blocks schedule approval and copy action.
- Haraj account name is dynamic inside the generated ad copy.

WORDPRESS BRIDGE
- MZJ Haraj Ad Data Bridge v1.1.0
- Read-only endpoint /wp-json/mzj-haraj/v1/cars
- Reports compare_key_status + compare_key_found.
